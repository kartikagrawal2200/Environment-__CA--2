10-Step Action Plan for LPU

A concrete, staged roadmap designed specifically for Lovely Professional University faculty, administrative wings, and students to target a 65% reduction in physical paper consumption over 24 months. Click items to check them off as completed.

Implementation Progress 0 / 10 Completed
✓
01.

Mandate Digital Assignment & Form Submissions

Transition all continuous assessment (CA) homework and official administrative requests exclusively to the University Management System (UMS) portal.

✓
02.

Enforce Double-Sided Duplex Printing by Default

Configure all university-managed campus printing kiosks, library terminals, and department printers to default automatically to duplex double-sided output.

✓
03.

Replace Printed Circulars with QR Code Displays

Replace paper event flyers and bulletin notices with permanent digital screen kiosks and scannable QR display stands leading to mobile notices.

✓
04.

Standardize Cloud LMS Lecture Handouts

Prohibit mandatory physical purchases of printed course packs; provide all lecture slide decks, lab rubrics, and reference texts in searchable PDF/ePub.

✓
05.

Install Dedicated Clean-Paper Recycling Receptacles

Position blue paper recycling bins directly beside every photocopier and academic block exit, strictly segregated from food and drink waste.

✓
06.

Procure Certified 100% Recycled Paper for Exams

Shift institutional bulk procurement contracts to post-consumer recycled chlorine-free paper for essential examination answer sheets and hall passes.

✓
07.

Incentivize Digital Note-Taking & Tablets

Provide student discounts for e-ink tablets and stylus hardware, accompanied by academic workshops on cloud note organization.

✓
08.

Conduct "Think Before You Print" Awareness Campaigns

Affix visual reminders on computer lab print dialogs reminding users of the freshwater and carbon cost of unneeded pages.

✓
09.

Deploy a Public Campus Sustainability Dashboard

Publish monthly ream consumption data and trees saved per faculty department on campus portal dashboards to stimulate friendly competition.

✓
10.

Establish Certified E-Waste Collection & Safe Disposal

Partner with state-authorized electronic recyclers to ensure decommissioned campus hardware, spent toner cartridges, and batteries are recycled safely.
