Transparency & Ethics

Privacy Policy & Data Ethics

How user information, pledges, and local device storage are treated across this academic platform.

1. Zero Third-Party Tracking

This research website is completely free of third-party advertising trackers, profiling pixels, commercial data brokers, and biometric scripts. We do not sell, rent, or monetize student or visitor data under any circumstances.

2. Browser LocalStorage Usage

To maintain responsive speed and work without server-side database requirements, certain user interactions are saved locally within your own browser via HTML5 localStorage:

  • Theme Preference: Saves whether you selected dark mode or light mode (theme).
  • Action Plan Progress: Remembers which of the 10 LPU action items you checked off (lpu_plan_state).
  • Pledge & Messages: Keeps a local confirmation copy of your pledge email and submitted feedback.

This data remains strictly stored on your own device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser cache.

3. Academic Citation & Fair Use

All statistical indicators from the FAO, WWF, and US EPA are utilized strictly under academic fair use for non-commercial environmental educational purposes.

4. Contact for Inquiries

For questions regarding data ethics or university sustainability guidelines, please reach out via our Contact Page.
