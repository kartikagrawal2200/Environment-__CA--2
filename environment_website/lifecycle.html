From Forest Harvest to Landfill Fate

The Life-Cycle of a Single Sheet of Paper

Paper seems organic and harmless on our desks, but its cradle-to-grave trajectory consumes tremendous water, harsh chemicals, energy, and fossil-fueled freight. Explore all 7 industrial phases below.

Phase 1 of 7

Forest Harvesting & Timber Clearing

Mature trees (such as eucalyptus, pine, and birch) are felled. Over 40% of all industrial timber harvested globally enters the paper manufacturing supply chain, contributing to local soil erosion and disruption of native wildlife canopies.

Canopy & Biodiversity Depletion

Stage Environmental Balance

Every metric ton of virgin printer paper requires approximately 17 to 24 mature trees, completely removing their ongoing carbon sequestration capacity.

Where Does Paper Pile Up in Daily Life?

Most paper wastage occurs not from deliberate malice, but through outdated administrative inertia and unoptimized defaults.

CAMPUSES & UNIVERSITIES

Academic Workflows

  • • Lab manuals printed once and discarded
  • • Exam hall admission tickets & answer booklets
  • • Notice boards cluttered with paper flyers
  • • Single-sided assignment printouts
  • • Unused supplementary course handouts
CORPORATE OFFICES

Administrative Operations

  • • Multi-page contracts printed for a single signature
  • • Paper invoices lost between departments
  • • Duplicate photocopying of employee files
  • • Physical receipt slips and expense dockets
  • • Paper filing cabinets consuming real estate
COMMERCE & RETAIL

Packaging & Marketing

  • • Direct mail brochures that go straight to bins
  • • Thermal paper receipts coated in toxic BPA
  • • Single-use promotional packaging inserts
  • • Event programs and catalogs
  • • Non-recyclable glossy laminated print runs
