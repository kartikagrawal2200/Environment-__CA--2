About the Research & Team

Developed as part of the Environmental Studies Continuous Assessment 2 (CA-2) at Lovely Professional University, Punjab, India.

While society has entered a hyper-connected digital age, global paper manufacturing remains at historic highs—consuming more than 420 million metric tons annually. This project investigates why paper consumption persists, calculates its true ecological price on water tables and atmospheric carbon, and formulates an actionable 10-step institutional transition plan for Lovely Professional University.

Core Research Objectives

Obj 1
Quantify Life-Cycle Externalities:

Synthesize authoritative data on freshwater withdrawal, chemical bleaching effluent toxicity, timber harvest volumes, and landfill methane production.

Obj 2
Deliver an Objective Matrix:

Avoid simplistic 'paper bad, screen good' assumptions by systematically examining electronic waste generation, rare earth mining, and server power draw.

Obj 3
Formulate LPU Campus Action Plan:

Translate environmental science findings into an actionable 10-step campus policy framework incorporating duplex defaults, LMS integration, and certified e-waste handling.

Obj 4
Engage the Community Interactively:

Provide an interactive personal footprint calculator and sustainability quiz to foster voluntary conservation behaviors among students and staff.
